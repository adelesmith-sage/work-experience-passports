A way to measure independence

FROM WATCHING TO OWNING

Use this ladder for ticket triage, access requests and later documentation or partner tasks. Move up only when the task and access are appropriate; review still matters.

Watch

See the task done. Ask what the person checked and why.

Do together

Take a step with your buddy beside you. Write down the process.

Lead, then check

Prepare the work yourself. Ask for review before any live change or external reply.

Own the loop

Choose the right guide, complete a suitable task, check it, update people and log the learning.

Habits to make routine

Tick each habit when you can show it on a suitable task.

When to bring someone in

Ask your buddy or manager when the guide is unclear, the task touches live data or credentials, or you are unsure who owns the issue. In the workbook's routing guide: environment data issues go to CloudOps; feature timing or pricing goes to Product; broken systems go to Engineering through the proper ticket route.

Knowing when to ask is part of independent work.
