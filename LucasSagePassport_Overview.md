# Lucas's Sage Enablement Passport: page overview

[Open Lucas's passport](https://adelesmith-sage.github.io/work-experience-passports/LucasSagePassport.html) · [Open Lucas's Confluence onboarding hub](https://confluence.sage.com/spaces/SFLOC/pages/916586929/Lucas)

## Purpose

The passport gives Lucas, an Apprentice Enablement Engineer in Sage Domain Services, a practical path from his start on **5 October 2026** through his first six months. It turns training into small pieces of work, conversations with the teams he will depend on, and evidence of growing independence.

The page works alongside two existing resources:

- The **First Month Workbook** guides Weeks 1–4 through setup, safe working, shadowing, ticket triage, access requests, a Confluence update, reflection, and sign-off.
- **Lucas's Confluence pages** hold the detailed training tracker, target dates, confidence scores, learning resources, and daily learning log. The passport links to the hub so Lucas can find the current page without copying its full course list.

## What the page contains

The sticky navigation has six stops:

| Section | What Lucas uses it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Start** | See where the workbook, Confluence, and passport fit; try the weekly rhythm of choosing a task, doing it with the right support, and logging what he learned. |
| **Your route** | Follow six phases from Week 1 through Month 6, each with concrete activities and a short prompt about what to show at a check-in. |
| **People** | Book short introductory meetings, mark invitations and meetings complete, and record one useful takeaway. |
| **Independence** | Move from watching a task to doing it together, leading with review, and owning a suitable task end to end. |
| **Reflect** | Capture a short Month 1, Month 3, and Month 6 review: what he can do now and what to practise next. |
| **Next steps** | Return to this week's Confluence page, book the next conversation or practice slot, and agree the next task to own. |

### Introductory meetings

Lucas is prompted to send the invitations himself and can adapt a short message provided on the page. Adele helps identify the right person where the source material names a role rather than an individual.

| Stakeholder | Suggested timing | What Lucas should learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lee, his manager | Week 1 | What good Month 1 progress looks like and how to raise a blocker. |
| Adele, his mentor | Week 1 | The check-in rhythm, suitable practice tasks, and review points. |
| An L1 support colleague | By Week 3 | What makes a useful handover to L2. |
| His apprenticeship coach | Month 1 | Review dates, learning records, and portfolio evidence. |
| A CloudOps colleague | By Month 2 | How environment or data issues reach CloudOps. |
| A Product Manager | By Month 2 | Where product decisions and roadmap questions belong. |
| An Engineering contact | By Month 2 | How technical issues are routed and what evidence helps investigation. |

Each meeting card has **Invited** and **Met** checkboxes plus space for one takeaway. The page suggests 15–20 minute conversations and one question to bring to each.

### Growing independence

The four-step ladder is **Watch → Do together → Lead, then check → Own the loop**. It applies first to ticket triage and access requests, then to suitable documentation or partner work. A companion checklist asks Lucas to find the guide, check the environment and permissions, get any required review, update the requester, and record the outcome in the right system. It also explains when to ask a buddy or manager and how CloudOps, Product, and Engineering fit into escalation.

## How progress and notes work

The progress bar counts completed passport checkboxes. Text notes and checkboxes save automatically in the current browser. **Export** downloads a JSON backup; **Import** restores one after asking before replacing the saved answers. There is no account sync, so Lucas should use the same browser or keep an approved backup. The page can also be printed.

The HTML is hosted on GitHub Pages. Lucas should keep customer details, ticket contents, passwords, keys, and other personal information out of the passport and its exports. Practice belongs in the sandbox, and work evidence belongs in approved Sage systems. The Confluence learning log remains the main record for apprenticeship learning.
